## 1. Delay
context on what the video is about is delayed for too long in the video - 단 두 문장의 딜레이도 딜레이임

## 2. Confusion
### Becoming a better writer
- use fewer words, but enough that the viewer can't misunderstand you
- use simpler words, ideally at a 6th grade reading level
- use direct active voice instead of passive voice on the sentences so it's easier to understand : 수동태X 능동태O

### 수정 prompt
>I've written a hook for a short-form video about {topic}. Increase the clarity and the framing of the sentences I used. I want meaning to be the exact same, but rewrite this in a 6th grade reading level so there's no misunderstanding from the viewer

### hook에 대해 다음 질문하기:
   - Is it possible for the viewer to understand what I'm saying in the wrong way?
   - Is there more than one way these sentences could be interpreted?
## 3. Irrelevance
The viewer is not confident that what's coming next in the video after the hook is gonna be valuable enough to help them solve a problem.

### Say "you/your" instead of "me/I" in the hook

### Frame hook around Expected Value
- X: These are 3 common trends in skincare.
- O: If you struggle with acne, try these 3 things.

## 4. Disinterests
Build a curiosity loop - context -> new question -> context -> new question -> ....

Contrast: distance between the current common belief of the viewer and some contrarian or alternative perspective that you offer. Compare A(something they know) vs B(something they don't know, solves problem better, faster, cheaper)
